rclone-sync

A personal-use tool that keeps one folder on my computer in sync with my own Google Drive, using rclone and the Google Drive API.

What it does

  • Connects to my own Google Drive account with my own OAuth client (this app).
  • Uploads new or changed files from my computer to my Google Drive, and downloads changes made elsewhere (phone, web) back to my computer.
  • Runs automatically on a timer, roughly every 15 minutes, on my personal computer only.

Who uses it

Only me — a single personal user, on my own machine, on my own Drive account. It is not offered to the public, has no sign-up, and is not installed by anyone else.

Privacy

See the Privacy Policy. Summary: no data is collected, stored, shared or sold by this application — files stay between my computer and my own Google Drive.
